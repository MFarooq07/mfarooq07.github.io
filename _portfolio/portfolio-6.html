--- title: "Gesture-Controlled Robot" excerpt: "Gesture-Controlled Robot: Real-time hand motion tracking for intuitive robotic navigation.
" collection: portfolio --- An embedded robotics project that translates physical human gestures into real-time robot locomotion and control commands. The system utilizes motion sensors and computer vision algorithms to capture hand orientations, process kinematic data, and wirelessly transmit directional motor control signals to a microcontroller-driven robotic chassis for intuitive, hands-free navigation.
