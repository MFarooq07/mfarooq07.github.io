In Pakistan the elections results are still announced after manual tabulation. This results in delays in election result depending on how much people are involved in the process of counting votes, how much budget is available to support this process etc.

Creating an electronic machine to do the same job would be more efficient and would relatively be much faster with predicted time for when the results will be out. Click [here](https://github.com/MFarooq07/Electronic-Voting-Machine-EVM) to access the code and the project
